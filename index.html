Step 1: Color basics

Color terminology

Six terms that describe any color.

Hue

The parent color in its purest, fully saturated form, no white or black added. The true version of any color on the spectrum.

Tint

A hue with white added. Raise the lightness in your design tool to get a lighter version.

Shade

A hue with black added. Lower the brightness to get a darker version.

Tone

A hue with grey added. It is less vivid than the original and feels more muted.

Value

How light or dark a color looks, whatever its hue.

Saturation

The purity and intensity of a color. High saturation is vivid and bold. Low saturation reads as muted, desaturated, or grey.

Step 3: Color harmonies

Color theory & harmonies

In visual arts, there are various attempts to explain what colors go together. These are known as color theory. A basic concept is color harmony: a set of colors that work well together. Think of harmonies as the building blocks, or the underlying template, of a color palette.

Creating a color palette
